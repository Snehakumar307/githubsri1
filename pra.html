Vijay Deverakonda recently got married to Rashmika Mandanna in a private ceremony. Their wedding took place on 26 February 2026 at a luxury resort near Udaipur, known for its beautiful hills and royal wedding venues. The couple kept the ceremony very intimate, inviting only close family members and a few friends. They followed both traditions during the wedding: a traditional Telugu ceremony for Vijay’s family and a Kodava-style ceremony for Rashmika’s family culture. Before the wedding, pre-wedding events like mehendi and other rituals were held at the same resort. After the wedding, the couple hosted a grand reception in Hyderabad, where many film celebrities attended to celebrate their marriage.
